Scaffold is a six-week accelerator for women building products for the creative economy. Twenty come in with something already in their hands. The strongest leave investment-ready, with a pitch in front of people who can back them.

The moment

The tools of the creative economy are being rebuilt.
Women are building them, with almost no capital behind them.

Scaffold exist to close the gap for a small group of women at a time, and do it properly

A new generation of products is being made for how creative work gets done, how it gets seen, and how it gets paid for. Women are among the people building them, often as the founder, often on a laptop, often alone.

The support thins out exactly where it matters most. Early-stage women in the creative space are the least likely to reach the funding, the readiness, and the networks that turn a working product into a company. In 2025, female-founded startups took just ten percent of the equity funding raised across Africa, and male-founded companies raised, on average, several times more.

What Scaffold is

A six-week accelerator for women already building

Scaffold takes in up to twenty women who are building a product for the creative economy and already have something real, a working version, early users, a first version of the idea made tangible. Over six focused weeks, we help them sharpen the product, understand their market, and get ready to raise. At the end, we recognise the five strongest, and the whole cohort pitches to investors on demo day.

It is deliberately small. We would rather go deep with a few women than spread ourselves thin across many.

What the name means

A scaffold is the structure that holds a builder while she builds, and comes down once the thing can stand on its own. That is the role we play. We are not the product. We are what goes up around her while she makes it.

Who should apply

This is for you if you are already building.

Scaffold is for women building a product for the creative economy. That means a product, not only an idea. You do not need funding, a team, or traction to apply, but you do need to have started making the thing.

Criteria 1

You are a fit if

  • You are a woman building a product, platform, or tool for the creative economy.
  • You already have a working version, even a rough one, or real early users.
  • You are serious about turning what you have built into a business.
  • You can commit to six weeks of focused work.
Criteria 2

This is probably not the right moment if

  • You have an idea but have not started building yet.
  • You are looking for a grant with no programme attached.
  • You cannot give the six weeks the time they need.
How it works

Six weeks built
backwards from the pitch

Apply

Applications open in January 2027. Tell us what you are building and where you are with it. We read every one.

Get selected

We choose up to twenty women on clear signals: you are already building, you have something real, and you are ready to move.

Build (weeks one to four)

Focused sessions on product, your market, pricing, and telling your story, with mentors who have done it and a success manager keeping you on track. You test what you are building with real audiences and come back with what you learned.

Get ready to raise (weeks five to six)

An investment-readiness track, run with our partner Frontier Access, so that what you show on pitch day is prepared, not raw.

Pitch

The cohort pitches to investors and partners. The strongest founders are backed, and every founder leaves in front of people who can take them further.

The award and demo day

The strongest five are backed. Everyone leaves in the room.

Scaffold takes in up to twenty women. At the end of the six weeks, we name the five strongest founders, and we are raising a grant pool to back them, given with no equity and no claim on your company. Every founder in the cohort pitches on demo day, in front of investors and partners who came to look. That is the real prize: the chance to be seen, and backed, by the people who can fund what comes next.

Who is behind it

For Creative Girls!

Scaffold is run by For Creative Girls, a community that has backed women in creative fields since 2017. In that time we have delivered more than a thousand mentoring sessions, mentored 180 women one to one, seen seventy percent of them move into jobs, placements, or shown work, and grown a community of more than ten thousand across fifteen countries.

This is our sourcing advantage. When a founder struggles to find credible women builders in this space, our community is the answer.

The readiness track is run with Frontier Access which prepares founders to raise. Scaffold founders arrive on demo day ready, not raw.
